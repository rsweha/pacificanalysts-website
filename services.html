2. Prove it in four weeks

Whichever you pick, it runs the same way. The AI Proof-of-Value Pilot is two focused hours a week for a fixed fee, with a champion on your team who does the hands-on work between sessions.

Week 1

Agree the number

Confirm the workflow is the right one, record where you are today, and set what would count as success.

Week 2

Test on real data

Run it against examples your team has already handled, and see where it gets them right and wrong.

Week 3

Put it to work

Your champion starts using it on live work, with a person approving every action.

Week 4

Keep it or drop it

You see the results against the number. If it earned its place, your team is already using it.

The pilot reads and drafts. It does not send, pay, or commit on its own. And because your champion helps build it, the know-how stays with your team.

3. Expand from a win

Once the first pilot has earned its place, there are two ways forward. You choose, and you can do one and then the other.

Do more pilots

When you want the next win.

Pick another workflow and run the same four weeks. Each one adds a win, and more people on your team who know how to do it.

Re-imagine the business

When you're ready to change how the business runs.

We step back from single workflows and redesign how work moves through the business: what agents do, what people decide, and what data connects it. Then we put it in place one proven step at a time.

One method behind all of it

We call it DRIVE. A pilot runs a fast Rank and Validate on one workflow. Re-imagining the business runs all five, starting with the data, where most AI work stalls.

Diagnose

Find what is real and what is aspirational.

Rank

Choose by value and feasibility.

Integrate

Connect the data and systems agents need.

Validate

Prove it moves the number.

Embed

Make it how the work runs.
